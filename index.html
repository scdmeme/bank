The Jotchua Banking System

How the bank pays its depositors

A single, transparent cycle runs the house. Three steps, repeated continuously — fees in, buybacks out, dividends home.

I.

Fees accrue

Every $JOTCHUA trade generates a creator fee. 100% of it flows straight into the Bank's reserve — the vault that funds everything below.

II.

The bank buys back

On a fixed cadence the reserve market-buys $JOTCHUA from the open market — steady, automatic demand funded by the house, not promises.

III.

Dividends to holders

Bought-back tokens are distributed pro-rata to qualifying holders — interest paid to depositors. Keep your account open for at least 10 minutes to receive your dividends.
